Is K.NETWORKING a real bank? +

No. This is a fictional website concept for design and learning. It does not provide regulated banking services or hold customer funds.

Can I open a real account here? +

No real account can be opened. The registration page demonstrates front-end form validation only.

Are the balances and statistics real? +

No. Balances, statistics, testimonials, and transactions are fictional demonstration content.

Will the contact form send a message? +

Not yet. It displays a demo response. Connect a form service or backend to receive messages.
