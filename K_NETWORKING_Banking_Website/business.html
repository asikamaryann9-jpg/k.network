Quick answers about this demonstration website.

Are these live banking services?+

No. These are educational descriptions and interface examples, not operational financial products.

Can I transfer money here?+

No. This website has no banking connection and cannot move money, accept deposits, or process payments.

Are the displayed figures real?+

No. All balances, statistics, and sample customer content are fictional.

How do I use a real financial service safely?+

Visit the official website or app of a legitimate provider and never share passwords, PINs, or one-time verification codes.

Can I send a message through the website?+

The contact form shows a local demonstration response. It needs a configured form service or backend to deliver messages.
